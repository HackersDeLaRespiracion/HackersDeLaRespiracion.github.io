Recommended reading

01

Organ Speak: What It Really Means to listen to Our Bodies

Giulia Enders · Greystone Books

This book changed the way I see myself, through Giulia Enders' clear and refreshingly humorous explanations of human biology. You'll discover that your body isn't an opaque mystery — it's an extraordinarily intelligent system that often already knows what it needs. If you've been curious about what actually happens inside you every time you breathe, this book gives you a solid and surprising foundation to build on.

02

Neuroscience of the Body (currently available in Spanish)

Nazaret Castellanos · Editorial Kairós SA

Nazaret Castellanos demonstrates in this book that the body is not simply a support structure for the mind: it actively shapes how we think, feel and make decisions. Drawing on the latest neuroscience, she explores how posture, breathing and physical sensations mould our inner experience. After reading it, I looked at my own breathing in a completely different way.
