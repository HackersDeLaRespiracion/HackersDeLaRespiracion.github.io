01

Tao Te Ching for Living (currently available in Spanish)

Ángel García, Daniel García y Liu Zheng · Anaya Multimedia

There is a wisdom that cannot be taught — only remembered. The Tao has been pointing to what neuroscience now confirms for millennia: that calm is not achieved, it is allowed. This book is an invitation to let go. To breathe from stillness, to move from centre. Its pages won't give you step-by-step instructions — the Tao never works that way — but they will open an inner space for you to find your own rhythm.

02

The Presence Process

Michael Brown · Namaste Publishing

Some books are read; others ask you to do work. This is the second kind. Michael Brown offers something apparently simple — being present — and spends his pages explaining with extraordinary clarity why it is so hard. His proposal is a ten-week process whose central commitment is to breathe consciously for fifteen minutes twice a day. No gadgets, no guru. Just you, your breath, and a weekly affirmation that activates something difficult to describe and easy to feel.
